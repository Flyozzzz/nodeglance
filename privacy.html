Nodeglance Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 20, 2026

Nodeglance does not collect any data about you. It has no account, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking, and it sends nothing to the developer.

Your servers

Nodeglance connects directly from your device to the servers and Beszel hubs you add. Server addresses, user names and the pinned SSH host keys are stored on your device only. Passwords, private keys and tokens are stored in the system Keychain of your device and are never uploaded or synced.

Commands and output

Metrics, Docker lists, logs and console output are read from your server and kept in memory on your device. They are not sent anywhere else.

Agent access on Mac

If you turn on local agent access, an app you choose on your Mac can talk to Nodeglance through a local MCP helper. It only reaches the servers you allow, every command needs your approval, and SSH credentials are never given to the agent.

Purchases

The optional Pro purchase is handled entirely by Apple through the App Store, under Apple’s privacy policy. Nodeglance receives only whether Pro is unlocked.

Contact

Questions: see the support page.

Политика конфиденциальности

Nodeglance не собирает никаких данных о вас. Нет аккаунта, аналитики, рекламы и отслеживания, разработчику ничего не отправляется. Приложение подключается напрямую к добавленным вами серверам и хабам Beszel. Адреса и ключи хостов хранятся только на устройстве, пароли и ключи — в Связке ключей и никуда не передаются. Доступ агентов на Mac работает локально, только к выбранным серверам и с одобрением каждой команды. Покупка Pro проходит через App Store.
